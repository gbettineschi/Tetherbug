**macOS**

- Hotspot shutdown cooldowns are now 5 minutes, including a phone-side On-to-Off transition received while the Tetherbug Bluetooth link remains connected. Bluetooth loss or an Off state first seen after reconnecting does not start a cooldown.
- Click Automatic Mode to disable it without a deadline; click again to resume. The checkmark appears only while activation is active.
- Turn off automatic mode for… offers 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours, Today, and Indefinitely. Today ends at the next local midnight. A new duration replaces the old deadline instead of adding to it.
- Timed suspensions show Turned off until… beside Automatic Mode. Indefinitely disables the mode without an expiry label. Both menu entries remain available while the mode is off.
- Automatic cooldowns preserve a longer selected suspension. Manual hotspot On cancels a timed suspension but leaves disabled automatic mode off.
- The native checkmark gutter remains reserved, so changing the checkmark does not shift command labels. The menu-bar icon stays stable while waiting for the phone.
