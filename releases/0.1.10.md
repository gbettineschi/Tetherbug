**macOS**

- Suspend Bluetooth and hotspot work before sleep and throughout DarkWake. A normal wake reconnects and reads fresh phone status. Queued requests from before sleep are discarded.
- Use the macOS-managed background service when Launch at Login is enabled. If a session worker is already running, it hands over to the registered service. Settings offers a retry when macOS cannot start the service.

**Android (versionCode 110)**

- Version metadata only. The APK keeps the same signing identity and is published through the existing Obtainium channel. The Mac fixes also work with Android 0.1.9.

**Validation**

- 65 native macOS regression tests. On a Mac paired with a Galaxy S23, the registered worker turned the hotspot on and off. A lid-close test captured a DarkWake with Bluetooth suspended and no hotspot request, followed by a successful reconnect at normal wake. Wi-Fi was available before and after that test.

The Mac app is signed ad hoc. If macOS refuses to start the background service after updating, open Tetherbug from Applications and turn **Launch at Login** off and back on.
