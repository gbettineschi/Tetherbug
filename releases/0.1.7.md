**macOS**

- A fresh phone hotspot On-to-Off transition received while Tetherbug Bluetooth remains connected pauses automatic activation for 15 minutes.
- Wi-Fi loss, Bluetooth loss, and an Off state first discovered after Bluetooth reconnection do not trigger this cooldown.
- The persisted deadline appears beside Automatic mode. A manual On from the Mac cancels it; repeated Off reports cannot keep postponing it.
- Android does not report the shutdown cause. Both manual shutdown and a native idle timeout can satisfy the connected-nearby rule.
